ADD THESE TWO LINES TO backend/app/main.py AFTER your existing imports:

from .admin_features import router as admin_router
app.include_router(admin_router)

IMPORTANT:
Because admin_features imports current_user and audit from main.py, put the two lines
AFTER current_user and audit have been defined, or refactor those helpers into a separate
auth module. The simplest safe approach is:

1. Leave the imports at the top.
2. Add `from .admin_features import router as admin_router`
   immediately before `@app.get("/")`
3. Add `app.include_router(admin_router)` immediately before `@app.get("/")`

If Python reports a circular import, instead move `current_user`, `role`, and `audit`
from main.py into a separate `auth_dependencies.py`, then import them from there.
